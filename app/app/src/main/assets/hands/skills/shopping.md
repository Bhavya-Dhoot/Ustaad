1. Open the app. Close pop-ups with X or "Not now". If a login screen appears, stop and tell the person.
2. Tap the search bar (usually "Search for products") and type the item. Tap a result.
3. If a size or colour is needed, use the one the person named. If none was named, stop and ask.
4. Tap Add to Cart, Add to Bag or ADD TO BAG. The wording differs by app.
5. Check: the cart or bag icon shows a count, or the cart opens.
6. Open the cart and read the items, price and delivery date.
7. Stop at the cart and tell the person the items and total. Never tap Buy Now or Place Order.
