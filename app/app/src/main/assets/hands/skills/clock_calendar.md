1. Close pop-ups with X or "Not now". Use the app the person named: Clock for alarms, Calendar for events.
2. Alarm: tap Alarm, then + (Add alarm). Set the time and tap Save, OK or Set.
3. Check: the new alarm is in the list with its switch on.
4. Event: tap + or Create, then Event. Type the title (usually "Add title").
5. Set the date and time, check the day is the one asked, then tap Save.
6. If a sign-in or account screen appears, stop and tell the person.
7. Stop once it is saved and tell the person the time. If guests are added, stop before Save or Send: invitations go out.
