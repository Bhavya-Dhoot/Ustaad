1. Open the app. Close pop-ups and premium offers with X or "Not now". Tap Skip when an ad offers it.
2. Tap the search icon (a magnifier, usually at the top) and type the song, show or video. Tap the match.
3. Tap the play triangle if it does not start by itself.
4. Check: the progress bar moves and a pause icon shows.
5. If a sign-in, age check or subscription screen appears, stop and tell the person.
6. Stop once it plays and tell the person what is playing. Never tap Subscribe, Go Premium, Buy, Rent or Download.
