1. Open the app. Close pop-ups with X or "Not now". If it asks for an address, stop and tell the person.
2. Tap the search bar (usually "Search for products") and type one item. Pick the closest match.
3. Tap ADD on the product. Use + only if the person asked for more than one.
4. Check: usually ADD turns into a counter (- 1 +) and a cart bar shows the count.
5. Repeat steps 2 to 4 for each item. Note any item not found.
6. Tap the cart bar or Cart and read the items and total.
7. Stop at the cart. Tell the person the items, missing items and total. Paying is the person's tap.
