1. Open the app. Close pop-ups with X or "Not now". If a location prompt has no close, stop and tell the person.
2. Tap the search bar (usually "Search for dishes") and type the dish or restaurant. Tap the result.
3. Tap Add, ADD or + on the item. Pick options only if the person named them.
4. Check: a cart bar or "View cart" shows the item count.
5. Tap View Cart or Cart and read the items and total.
6. If a login, sign-in or OTP screen appears, stop and tell the person. Never type a code.
7. Stop at the cart and tell the person the items and total. Paying is the person's tap.
