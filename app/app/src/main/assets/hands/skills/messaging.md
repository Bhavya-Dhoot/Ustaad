1. Open the app. Close pop-ups with X or "Not now". If a login, QR or link-device screen appears, stop and tell the person.
2. Find the chat: search for the name, or tap it in the list. Google Messages usually has a Start chat button. If the contact is not found, stop and tell the person.
3. Check the name at the top is the contact asked for. If not, go back.
4. Tap the box that says "Type a message" and type the text.
5. Check the text is in the box. Do not tap Send yet.
6. Stop before Send and tell the person the name and the exact text. Sending is the person's tap.
