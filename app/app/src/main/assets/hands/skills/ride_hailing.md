---
1. Open the app. Close pop-ups with X or "Not now". If a location prompt has no such control, stop and tell the person.
2. Tap the drop-off box (usually "Where to?") and type the place. Tap the matching result.
3. Check the pickup point. Change it only if the person named another one.
4. Pick a vehicle such as Auto, Bike, Mini or Go. Read its fare and pickup time.
5. If a login, OTP or payment setup screen appears, stop and tell the person.
6. Stop before Book, Confirm or Book Ride. Tell the person the vehicle, fare and pickup time. Booking is the person's tap.
