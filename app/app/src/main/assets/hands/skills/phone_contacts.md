1. Open Phone or Contacts. Close pop-ups with X or "Not now". If it asks for contacts or phone access, stop and tell the person.
2. To find someone, tap the search bar (often "Search contacts") and type the name. Tap the contact.
3. Check the name and number are the ones meant. If there are two numbers, ask which one.
4. To save one, tap + or Create contact, type the name and number, then tap Save.
5. Tap the Call or phone icon only when the person asked for a call.
6. Stop once the call screen shows and tell the person. Never dial emergency numbers such as 112 yourself.
