# Hammer: the owner sets keep busy to full duty (2026-10-11)

Asked for by the founder at 23:45 on 2026-10-10 after the duty rule test (2026-10-10-throttle-passive.md section 7): the app should not choose between usage and speed, he does. Built as commit c8940c5, merged as f62da67, 1453 unit tests, 0 failures. Installed on Phone B at 00:06 and Phone C at 00:07.

## What it is
- A switch per phone, "Hammer". With it on, the keep-busy loop does not drop to half duty at Thermal Status 2: filler runs back to back on a warm phone too.
- Everything else stays ahead of it: real work goes first, the loop pauses while the phone is too hot (Thermal Status 3 or more) and carries on by itself when it is under 3 again, and it pauses under 30 % battery when not charging.
- It is not stored. It is off whenever Ustaad starts.
- When it is switched off the phone runs no filler for 90 seconds, then normal keep busy continues. Real work is not held by that rest. Switching Hammer on again ends the rest at once. Switching Keep busy itself off turns Hammer off without a rest.
- Turning Hammer on turns Keep busy on.
- `GET /v1/sched/busy` reports `hammer`; the state during the rest is `hammer_cooldown`. A debug build takes `--ez hammer true|false` on the activity.

## Where it is
Two buttons in the Settings sheet, the same switch: the first item of the page, with the explanation under it, and the last item, above "Save changes". "Hammer: maximum usage" when off (outline), "Hammer is on. Tap to stop." when on (filled). Seen on Phone B at 00:08 in both places (`raw/gate-9b/hammer/settings-top.png`, `settings-bottom.png`).

## Checked on Phone B warm, 00:13 to 00:16 (one run)
Started when the phone had reached Thermal Status 2 and normal keep busy had dropped to half duty. Raw: `raw/gate-9b/hammer/check-B-0013-warm.txt`.

| | Seen |
|---|---|
| Before | Thermal Status 2, state half duty |
| Hammer on, 61 s | Thermal Status 2 throughout; state running from the second sample on (the first, 4 s after the switch, still showed half duty); 22 filler jobs, about four every 10 s; share of the last 60 s generating 94 to 98 % |

So on a warm phone Hammer holds full duty where the normal rule had gone to half, and the rest after it is the 90 seconds asked for. What it does to a phone over time is the next section.

## Hammer for 25 minutes on Phone A, 01:15 to 01:49 (one run)
Build f62da67, charging, Ustaad in front. Two minutes of normal keep busy, then Hammer on at 01:17:39 and off at 01:42:39, one real 48-token request every five minutes, the read-only watch every 10 s. Raw: `raw/gate-9b/hammer/A-long-watch.jsonl`, `A-long-real.log`, `A-long-switch.log`, and the figures below as computed in `A-long-analysis.txt`.

| | Normal keep busy, the two minutes before | Hammer, 25 minutes |
|---|---|---|
| Share of time generating filler | 46 % | 78 % (by five-minute block: 96, 79, 77, 72, 68 %) |
| Filler tokens per minute | 259 | 379 (468, 382, 373, 347, 333) |
| Filler jobs | 24, 0 failed | 437, 0 failed |
| Thermal Status | 2 | 2 in 79 % of samples, 3 in 21 %, never 4 |
| `gpu` throttle step | 5 | 7 from the second sample on |
| Skin temperature | 42.3 to 42.5 C | up to 45.0 C |
| Battery temperature | 39.5 to 39.6 C | up to 42.0 C |
| Shell temperature (`tz_shell`) | 39.6 to 39.8 C | up to 42.0 C |
| Real requests, decode | 13.3 tok/s (one request) | 10.8 to 11.1 tok/s (five requests), 0.05 to 0.25 s outside the model |

- What it buys: about 46 % more filler tokens per minute than normal keep busy and a share of time near 80 % where normal holds about half.
- What it costs: real work at the slow throttle level the whole time (10.9 tok/s against 13.3 before), which is the level normal keep busy also ends at once a phone has reached step 7, and a hotter phone: 45 C skin, 42 C battery.
- The pause for heat works on a phone. Thermal Status 3 was reached 6.4 minutes after switching on and then about every two and three quarter minutes; each time the loop paused for 31 to 51 s (seven pauses) and carried on by itself when the status was 2 again. Real requests sent during a pause were answered at the same speed. Thermal Status 4 was not reached.
- After switching off: no filler job for the 90 s of rest (the count stayed at 680), then half duty. In those 90 s the shell fell from 41.7 to 40.0 C and the skin from 44.7 to 42.3 C. The throttle step stayed at 7 through the rest and the five minutes watched after it, and a real request then decoded at 10.7 tok/s: 90 seconds are not enough to leave the slow step.

On the next build (fa8b5cd, Phone A, 01:51) "Stop Ustaad" was pressed with Hammer on: the button at the bottom of Settings changed back to "Hammer: maximum usage", and after "Start Ustaad" `GET /v1/sched/busy` reported `hammer` false.
